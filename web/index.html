AirGesture 3D

Hand-tracked AR notepad, liquid box and Rubik's cube. Tracking runs on this device.

  • Move a hand to the right edge (or tap the right handle) to open the sidebar
  • Pinch inside a box and drag to rotate it; rest an open palm on it (or make a fist) to move it
  • Pinch with both hands to resize; press it from both sides with open palms to burst it
  • Rubik's cube: swipe with only the index finger to turn a layer
  • Notepad: hold 2–3 fingers together and write letters in the air; spread them to stop (line → = space, line ← = delete)
  • Touch works too: drag, pinch, long-press to move, double-tap a box to burst it
  • Record saves the screen (camera + effects) as a video
